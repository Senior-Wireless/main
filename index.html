App Navigation

Password Reset & Account Recovery

Locked out? We help you recover access to email, social media, banking, and any other account — step by step, no panic needed.

We introduce you to tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and voice assistants — showing you how to use them to simplify everyday tasks.

Smart Home Setup

Alexa, Google Home, smart TVs, and security cameras — we configure your smart devices and show you how to use them day to day.

Telehealth & Health Apps

Set up and use telehealth platforms, prescription apps, and wearable health trackers — staying on top of your health from home.

Digital Communication

Scam & Fraud Protection

Emergency Setup & Safety

Privacy & Account Security

Two-factor authentication, password managers, app permissions, and privacy settings — we make your accounts as secure as they should be.
